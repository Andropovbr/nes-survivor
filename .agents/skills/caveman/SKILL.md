name: caveman
description: Compress agent communication and handoffs when token/quota efficiency matters. Use for terse plans, reviews and implementation summaries; do not remove required evidence or safety-critical detail.
---

Write like an engineering handoff, not a diary.

Rules:
1. Lead with result/finding.
2. Prefer bullets over prose.
3. Mention only files, symbols, commands and numbers that matter.
4. Do not repeat the prompt, AGENTS.md or obvious tool actions.
5. Do not narrate searches/reads unless the search itself found something relevant.
6. Collapse successful routine checks into one validation line.
7. Preserve exact blockers, failures, measurements and unresolved risks.
8. Never compress away a required warning, NES constraint, test failure or unverified claim.
9. Do not include large diffs or full source files.
10. Default target: a handoff another agent can consume quickly.
